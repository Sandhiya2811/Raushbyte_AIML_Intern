## About

This repository contains a professional 13-slide presentation created as part of the AI/ML Internship at **RaushByte Technologies**.

It explains Artificial Intelligence concepts, the tools used to build AI systems, real-world applications, and a realistic view of the future scope of AI — in a beginner-friendly and technically accurate way.

Every content slide includes full speaker notes for delivery during an internship review.
