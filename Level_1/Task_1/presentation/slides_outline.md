## Slide 2 — What is AI?

### On Slide

* AI = systems that perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence
* "The science and engineering of making intelligent machines" — John McCarthy (1956)
* Modern AI learns from data instead of following hand-written rules
* Traditional programming: **Rules + Data → Answers**
* Machine learning: **Data + Answers → Rules**

### Figure Reference

> 📊 **AI vs Traditional Programming vs Machine Learning**
> Source: `assets/diagrams/fig2_programming_vs_ml.png`

### Speaker Notes

The key idea is the direction of the arrow. Instead of a programmer writing every rule, we give the machine examples and it learns the rules itself.

## Slide 4 — AI in Healthcare

### On Slide

* Medical imaging — IDx-DR: first FDA-authorized autonomous AI diagnostic (2018)
* Drug discovery — AlphaFold: 200M+ protein structures, freely available
* Early warning — TREWS sepsis system (Johns Hopkins, *Nature Medicine* 2022)
* Monitoring — Apple Watch irregular-rhythm alerts (FDA-cleared)
* Key point: AI **assists** clinicians — it does not replace them

#### Speaker Notes

Emphasize regulation: healthcare AI requires validation, FDA clearance, and clinical oversight. Errors here have real clinical consequences.

## Slide 8 — Real-World Case Studies

### On Slide

Use four mini-cards. Each card should contain:

**Problem → Solution → Impact → Limitation**

### Case Studies

1. **DeepMind Retinal Screening**

   * Problem: Limited access to specialists
   * Solution: AI-based retinal disease detection
   * Impact: Supports screening and referral
   * Limitation: Requires clinical validation and appropriate deployment

2. **AlphaFold**

   * Problem: Protein structure prediction is computationally difficult
   * Solution: AI-based protein structure prediction
   * Impact: Large-scale protein structure database
   * Limitation: Predictions still require appropriate scientific interpretation and validation

3. **Khanmigo**

   * Problem: Need for scalable personalized tutoring
   * Solution: AI-powered tutoring assistance
   * Impact: Provides interactive learning support
   * Limitation: AI can generate incorrect information

4. **UPS ORION**

   * Problem: Complex delivery-route optimization
   * Solution: AI/data-driven route optimization
   * Impact: Reduces unnecessary driving
   * Limitation: Required years of development and significant investment



### Speaker Notes

Every case study has a limitation — that's deliberate. Honest reporting of limitations is a core part of this research task.
