## 🔑 Key Learnings

* AI systems **learn patterns from data** rather than following manually written rules — the fundamental difference between AI and traditional programming.
* AI is already **deployed in the real world**: DeepMind's retinal screening, AlphaFold protein prediction, Khanmigo AI tutoring, and UPS's ORION route optimization.
* In high-stakes fields, AI is **assistive, not autonomous** — doctors, teachers, and managers remain the final decision-makers.
* **Responsible AI** (fairness, transparency, privacy, human oversight) matters as much as technical capability.
* It is essential to **separate current capabilities from future possibilities** — impressive demos are not the same as reliable, deployed products.
